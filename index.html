Before you write

What does a project cost?

Every project is quoted from your brief. Send a short description and I’ll reply with a scope and a price.

Can I see a demo?

Yes, privately. The apps aren’t hosted publicly, so I show the working app live on a call.

What should I put in the brief?

What the system should do, who will use it, and when you need it. A rough idea is enough.

Can you work with my existing process?

Yes. We can start by reviewing your current workflow and identify what should be improved or automated before deciding the scope.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Post-launch support and maintenance can be discussed and included in the project proposal.
